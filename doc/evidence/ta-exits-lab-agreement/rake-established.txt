THE AGREEMENT -- the exits checks against the captain's own verdicts
Offline and free: every figure below is read off samples already bought.

AN ELIGIBLE VERDICT speaks to a check on a named place, counted once across
its draws. Good speaks to every judgeable per-name check; weak/bad speaks
through its aspects. shouldnt_exist speaks to every per-name check.
Whole-answer and detail checks are unavailable to a name judgement.
Below 30 eligible verdicts the counts are printed and no percentage is.
Tuning and held out are two sets and are never pooled.
