THE AGREEMENT

Whether the record checks agree with your per-name judgements. Each name counts once across its draws. Good speaks to every judgeable per-name check; weak or bad speaks through its aspects; shouldnt_exist speaks to all of them. A bare weak or bad can still be a miss. Whole-answer and detail checks are unavailable here. Below 30 eligible verdicts the fraction and both denominators are shown, and no percentage is. Tuning and held out stay apart. Offline and free: rake eval:exits_alignment prints the same evidence.
